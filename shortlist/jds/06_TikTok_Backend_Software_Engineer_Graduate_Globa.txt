TikTok is a short-form mobile video company with a global e-commerce business that enables users to discover and purchase products through video and live-commerce experiences. The Backend Software Engineer Graduate will develop scalable, secure backend services, APIs, data services, and platform components while supporting AI-powered product experiences and collaborating with cross-functional teams.

Responsibilities:
- Develop efficient, highly scalable and secure backend services that power E-commerce products and platform capabilities
- Optimize backend systems and services for reliability, modularity, computational efficiency, scalability, and data security
- Design and develop APIs, data services, and core platform components that support product innovation and business growth
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to deliver robust technical solutions for rapidly evolving product needs
- Apply AI-assisted development practices to improve engineering productivity, code quality, debugging efficiency, and service observability
- Build backend capabilities that support AI-powered product experiences, intelligent automation, and data-driven decision-making where applicable

Qualifications:
Must have:
- Individuals who are completing or have recently completed a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related discipline
- Familiarity with backend development and proficiency in at least one programming language such as Java, Golang, Python, Scala, or C++
- Understanding of data structures, algorithms, operating systems, computer networks, and scalable system design
- Familiarity with common databases, caching systems, or messaging frameworks such as MySQL, Redis, or Kafka
- Basic understanding of software security, privacy protection, and engineering best practices
Preferred:
- Strong problem-solving skills and a solid sense of ownership
- Demonstrated interest in building reliable platforms and large-scale internet services
- Familiarity with AI-assisted coding tools, engineering productivity workflows, or backend systems supporting intelligent applications
- Comfortable working in a fast-moving, collaborative, and globally distributed team environment
