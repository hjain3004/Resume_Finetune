TikTok develops short-form mobile video and operates TikTok Shop, where its Global E-Commerce Search Infrastructure team builds systems that power product discovery. The Software Engineer Graduate will design, implement, test, deploy, and operate components of large-scale search and distributed systems while contributing to search infrastructure, data pipelines, and AI-enabled search capabilities.

Responsibilities:
- Design, implement, test, deploy, and operate well-scoped components of large-scale search and distributed systems
- Build reliable online services and data pipelines with attention to latency, throughput, availability, freshness, cost, and maintainability
- Contribute to retrieval, indexing, query-processing, ranking infrastructure, or AI-enabled search capabilities
- Participate in technical design discussions, code reviews, documentation, testing, monitoring, and incident learning
- Use profiling, benchmarking, and production metrics to identify bottlenecks and drive measurable improvements
- Collaborate with search, machine-learning, product, and infrastructure partners to translate requirements into practical engineering solutions
- Learn from experienced engineers while progressively taking ownership of larger components and projects

Qualifications:
Must have:
- Individuals who are completing or have recently completed a Bachelor's/ Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related technical field
- Programming experience in at least one general-purpose language, such as C++, Go, Java, or Python
- Deep understanding of data structures, algorithms, and object-oriented or modular software design
- Familiarity with core software-engineering practices, including testing, debugging, version control, and code review
- Strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills
Preferred:
- Technical internships, research, open-source contributions, or substantial academic or personal projects
- Distributed systems, operating systems, networking, databases, storage systems, or low-latency services
- Search or information-retrieval concepts, such as inverted indexes, BM25, learning-to-rank, query understanding, vector search, FAISS, or HNSW
- Data-processing frameworks such as Kafka, Flink, or Spark
- AI or machine-learning infrastructure, model serving, retrieval-augmented generation, or agentic systems
- Performance profiling, Linux development, production debugging, competitive programming, or relevant publications
